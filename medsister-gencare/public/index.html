Consanguinity & Kinship Coefficient ($F$)

Consanguinity does not create new mutations. It simply increases the chance that both partners share the same ancestral recessive gene (*Identical by Descent*).

Inbreeding Coefficient ($F$): 6.25%
For first cousins, approximately 6.25% of their child's genome will be homozygous by descent. Premarital screening tests 570 genes to safeguard those specific shared regions.

Understanding Genetic Findings: Certainty vs Uncertainty

Pathogenic (P/LP)

Proven link to disease. If both partners carry this, couple concordance is flagged.

Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS)

DNA change has unknown effect. Never treated as diagnosis or reason for alarm. Monitored by counselors.

Benign (B/LB)

Harmless natural human variation. Completely safe.
